Organization members can receive vesting streams and view organization details.

## Member roles

| Role   | Permissions                                          |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner  | Full control: manage org, token, members, vesting    |
| Admin  | Can manage members (add/remove) and view org details |
| Member | Can view org details and receive vesting streams     |

## Adding a member

1. Go to your organization's **Members** section
2. Click **Add Member**
3. Enter the member's wallet address or user ID
4. Select a role (admin or member)
5. Click **Add**

Members must have signed in to SimplyVest at least once to appear in the member list.

## Changing roles

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click the role dropdown
3. Select the new role

Only owners can change member roles. You cannot change the owner's role.

## Removing a member

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click **Remove**
3. Confirm the action

Removing a member does **not** cancel their existing vesting streams — those continue
until they are claimed or manually cancelled.
